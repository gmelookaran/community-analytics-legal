Community Analytics

Privacy Policy

How the Adobe Global Student Community's read-only Discord analytics application processes server data.

Effective: October 2, 2026 · Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Who operates the application

Community Analytics (Discord application ID 1511096653933842594) is operated for the Adobe Global Student Community by administrators of that community. It is a first-party, read-only analytics application used in the community's Discord server.

This policy covers data processed by Community Analytics. Discord separately controls data it processes under Discord's own terms and privacy policy.

2. Scope and data sources

The application processes data available to its bot account in the Adobe Global Student Community server. This includes community channels and configured internal event-telemetry channels that the server administrators have made visible to the application. The application does not read direct messages and does not request the Guild Presences privileged intent.

Community Analytics may process the following categories:

  • Server and channel data: server and channel identifiers, names, types, hierarchy, icons, and approximate member counts.
  • Member data: Discord user ID, username, display name, avatar URL, bot status, server join date, current/departed status, and role assignments. Role history records the dates on which assignments were first or last observed, not the exact time Discord assigned a role.
  • Message and reaction data: message, channel, and author IDs; timestamps; message type; message text; notification mentions; emoji and reaction counts; and IDs of members who reacted.
  • Poll data: poll questions, answer text, counts, notification type, author and message metadata, and IDs of members who voted for each answer.
  • Community profile data: school, major or field of study, expected graduation year, and an extracted first or display name when members voluntarily include those details in introductions. A campus profile may also be imported from an authorized community program record after matching a voluntarily provided Discord tag; names and email addresses from that source are not stored by the importer.
  • Event data: scheduled-event names, channels, times, status and RSVP counts; RSVP subscriber IDs captured in observation snapshots; member IDs and timestamps for observed event joins, switches, and departures; and notification type and time for configured event announcements. Arrival timing is correlation only and does not identify a referral source.
  • Administrative records: giveaway winner and exclusion records, including member IDs, display names, related message links, and draw dates.

3. Why the data is processed

Community administrators use the data to operate and understand the community, including to measure membership, growth, engagement, reactions, polls, role participation, campus representation, onboarding, event attendance and RSVP conversion; prepare internal reports; support giveaways; and respond to member requests.

The operational basis for this processing is the community's need to administer a voluntary Discord program, evaluate whether its programming is useful, maintain longitudinal analytics, and protect the integrity of internal reports. This description is informational and is not legal advice or a statement about rights that may apply under every local law.

4. How information is presented and shared

The primary dashboard is hosted on an Adobe internal service protected by Adobe IMS authentication and intended for authorized staff. It presents aggregate metrics and may also present limited member-level records needed for operations, such as display names, avatars, join information, campus profile fields, activity counts, and excerpts and links for highly reacted server posts. It is not a public member directory.

Community Analytics does not sell Discord data or use it to profile people across servers. Public legal pages contain no member data. Data may be processed by service providers used to operate the workflow, including Discord, Adobe-hosted systems, geocoding services for school names, and—only when the optional assisted introduction parser is deliberately used—the configured text-analysis provider. Routine introduction parsing is performed locally.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Source records are stored in a local SQLite database controlled by the operator. Access to that database and to staff reporting is limited to people who need it for community operations. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used, but no storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

The 90-day limit applies only to raw text in the primary Discord message store. Message text is automatically blanked there after 90 days. It does not delete the corresponding message ID, author ID, channel ID, timestamp, message type, reaction records, or other activity metadata.

Other identifiers and metadata—including member records, role-observation history, parsed campus profile fields, poll-voter IDs, event action IDs, RSVP subscriber snapshots, giveaway records, and aggregate snapshots—may be retained longer for longitudinal analytics and request handling. Aggregated or de-identified statistics may be retained indefinitely.

A limited set of selected community quote excerpts may be copied into a separate internal archive with the associated display name, channel, date, and message ID so approved community-impact reporting can continue after the primary 90-day text purge. These excerpts are not published by the analytics dashboard and may be reviewed or removed in response to a valid request.

Discord may retain the source messages independently. Removing data from Community Analytics does not itself remove content from Discord.

6. Requests and choices

Members may ask to access, correct, or remove data associated with them by contacting the Adobe Global Student Community server administrators through the server's established administrator-contact route. Administrators will verify and evaluate the request and remove or de-identify locally held records where reasonably possible and appropriate. Some aggregate statistics may no longer be attributable to an individual and may remain.

7. Student community and age

The application supports a student-focused community but is not designed to determine a member's age. Participation remains subject to Discord's age requirements and the community's eligibility rules. Members should not post sensitive personal information in introductions or other server channels. If administrators learn that data was collected contrary to applicable age requirements, they can investigate and remove it through the request process above.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application, its data practices, or applicable platform requirements change. The effective and last-updated dates at the top identify the current version. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate community or application channel when feasible.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Adobe Global Student Community server administrators. No separate public email address is currently designated for this application.
